There are thoughts that refuse a desk. They arrive when the body is occupied — walking, lifting, watching a platform empty. They feel finished in the head and dissolve by the time a laptop opens. That is not a failure of memory so much as a failure of hospitality. The day had nowhere to put them.

I used to believe that an important thought would return. The important ones are often the least obedient. They do not book a second appointment. They want a hand on a recorder, or a ring you can press without taking out a phone, and later a quiet hour in which the recording is not treated as the work.

The commute, then, is not wasted time. It is a weather system. The studio is the place the weather is written down before it becomes climate, or before it becomes nothing.

Keep the raw note if you must. Do not confuse it with the piece. The piece is what you make when you are no longer moving.

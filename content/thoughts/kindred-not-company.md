I do not mean a search for a counterpart. I mean the quieter wish that a private weather is not private after all — that somewhere a person has stood in the same weather and will know it without being asked to applaud.

Most public writing now is built for reach. Reach is a poor instrument for this. Reach flattens a thought into a position. What I want is nearer to recognition: a reader who does not need the argument explained because they have already lived the premise.

That is a small audience by design. It may be no audience at all, for a long time. I still think it is the only honest reason to put the innermost work where a stranger can find it. Not to be followed. To leave a light on in a room that is usually locked.

If you are here and something sounds like your own mind, that is enough. You do not owe me a reply. The resonance is the reply.
